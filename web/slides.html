1. The Problem in 60 Seconds

Emergency Clinical Workflow

⚠️ The Clinical Bottleneck

  • • Delayed Radiologist Queues: ER patients wait hours for radiologist review during night shifts and surge hours.
  • • Asymmetric Risk: A false negative (missing a hairline fracture) leads to bone malunion, lifelong patient disability, and liability.
  • • Severe Imbalance: FracAtlas dataset has 4,083 scans with 4.7:1 imbalance (only 17.5% fractures).

🎯 Operational System Goal

  • • Instant Triage: Prioritize positive scans within <500ms to accelerate acute treatment.
  • • Failure Defense: Gracefully intercept corrupt PACS network streams without crashing.
  • • Visual Trust: Heatmap explainability highlighting where the fracture is located.

7. Monitoring, Observability & Drift Alerts

PSI & Kolmogorov-Smirnov

The 5 Dashboard Metrics

  • • Request Rate: Total requests and current requests/second
  • • Latency SLO: p50, p90, p95, p99 tracked under <500ms target
  • • Error Rate: HTTP 4xx/5xx failure ratios
  • • Confidence: Rolling mean, std deviation, and uncertainty ratio
  • • Drift Magnitude: Real-time PSI & KS statistic vs baseline

AlertManager with Cooldown

Sustained anomalies easily cause alert floods. Our AlertManager implements a 15-minute cooldown deduplication window. Repeated triggers during active incidents update status without spamming on-call staff.

Thresholds: PSI ≥ 0.20 · KS p < 0.05 · Error > 5%
